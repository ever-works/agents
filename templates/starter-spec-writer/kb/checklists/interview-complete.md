# Checklist: interview complete

Run this list before closing the interview and moving to drafting.
Every item is pass/fail. A fail means one more question to the
requester or an explicit Open question — never a guess.

## Coverage

- [ ] The opening restatement was confirmed or corrected by the
      requester.
- [ ] All six areas were walked in order: goals, users, use cases,
      non-goals, constraints, edge cases.
- [ ] Every goal has at least one use case, or the gap is recorded
      as an Open question.
- [ ] At least one non-goal is recorded. A spec with nothing ruled
      out has not been probed hard enough.
- [ ] Constraints capture what is fixed (deadlines, systems,
      compliance, prior decisions) as the requester stated them.

## Confirmation

- [ ] Every area was played back and answered with a confirm or a
      correction.
- [ ] Every correction was itself played back and confirmed.
- [ ] The full end-of-interview playback got an explicit confirm.
- [ ] No numbered item exists that the requester never saw.

## Contradictions

- [ ] Every contradiction noticed during the interview was raised as
      a flag with both answers quoted — none carried forward
      unresolved and none silently decided.
- [ ] Each resolution the requester chose is recorded next to the
      affected numbered items.

## Open questions

- [ ] Every "I don't know" became an Open question with a suggested
      owner.
- [ ] Parked threads ("good point, parking this") were either picked
      back up or recorded as Open questions.

## Boundaries

- [ ] No architecture, schema, technology, or estimate content
      anywhere in the notes.
- [ ] No invented users, metrics, or requirements — every item
      traces to a requester statement.

Every box checked: proceed to `playbooks/draft-spec-from-notes.md`.
One unchecked: back to the requester.
