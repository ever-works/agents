# Template: Contradiction flag

Use this the moment two answers conflict — mid-interview, during
drafting, or when a late answer contradicts the confirmed spec. One
flag per conflict. The interview or draft pauses until the requester
resolves it.

```
## Contradiction — <short label>

### What you said

1. <quote or faithful restatement of the earlier answer>
   (<where: interview area or spec item, e.g. "goals playback" or
   "G2">)
2. <quote or faithful restatement of the later answer>
   (<where>)

### Why these conflict

<one sentence: what cannot simultaneously be true. Name the numbered
items affected.>

### Options

- **A** — <keep answer 1 as stated>. Consequence: <which numbered
  items stay, change, or become non-goals>.
- **B** — <keep answer 2 as stated>. Consequence: <...>.
- **C** — <both, scoped: split by user segment, by phase, or by
  situation — only offer when it genuinely fits>. Consequence:
  <...>.

Which option — or is there a version I have not seen?
```

## Notes on filling it in

- Quote faithfully. The requester must recognise both answers as
  their own words; a strawman restatement poisons the resolution.
- The conflict sentence states an impossibility, not a preference.
  "UC4 requires a sales rep on every setup while G1 requires zero
  human contact" is a conflict; "these feel inconsistent" is not.
- Consequences are stated in spec terms only — which G, UC, NG
  items change. Never in implementation terms.
- Option C exists because many contradictions are really two
  segments or two phases talking past each other. But if the
  conflict is a genuine either-or, offer only A and B; a fake C
  wastes the requester's decision.
- End with exactly one question. The flag follows the same
  one-question rule as the rest of the interview.
- Record the chosen resolution in the spec's change log line and
  next to every affected numbered item.
