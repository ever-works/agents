# Playbook: Stalled deal revival

The deal went quiet. Decide whether to nudge once more, switch
channel, or close-lost cleanly. Never re-enrol a prospect who has
already said no.

## Trigger

A deal in stage "qualified" or later has had no prospect signal for
14 or more days. Pipeline review flagged it.

## Steps

1. **Read the last signal.** Open the most recent prospect message in
   full. Look for hedges ("not now", "circling back next quarter",
   "checking with my team") that suggest a future date, not a no.
2. **Check the opt-out state.** If the prospect ever replied with
   "stop", "unsubscribe", "remove", or equivalent — close-lost with
   reason "opted out" and stop. Do not re-enrol.
3. **Classify the stall.** Time stall (their reason is timing), trust
   stall (they need more proof), priority stall (they have other
   fires), or silence (no reason given).
4. **Pick one move:**
   - Time stall: schedule a single follow-up for the date they
     mentioned. No nudges before that date.
   - Trust stall: send one piece of new proof (case study, metric,
     security doc from KB). No second piece unless they reply.
   - Priority stall: ask one question — "should we pause this and
     pick up in 30 days, or close it out?". Let them choose.
   - Silence: send a single "should we close this thread?" line.
     If no reply within 7 days, close-lost with reason "silence".
5. **Update the CRM.** New next-step, new due date. If close-lost,
   record the reason verbatim.
6. **Add to the objection library** if a new objection class
   surfaced in the prospect's last message.

## Stop conditions

- Prospect has opted out.
- The deal is now over 90 days stalled — escalate to owner; do not
  send another nudge.
- The deal involves a contract under legal review — owner only.

## What good looks like

Stalled deals either move (date, proof, answer) or close cleanly
within two weeks. The pipeline shrinks honestly. No ghost deals
linger past 30 days.
