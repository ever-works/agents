# KB — Sales SDR

This knowledge base seeds the Sales SDR agent at create-time. Content
is specific to the qualifying-and-warming motion: ICP scoring, first-
touch outreach, objection handling, and CRM hygiene.

## Contents

- `playbooks/` — multi-step scenarios the agent follows. Two
  starters: handling a new inbound lead and reviving a stalled deal.
- `checklists/` — short pass/fail gates. Two starters: pre-send
  outreach check and weekly CRM hygiene check.
- `templates/` — output shapes. Two starters: the lead card and the
  three-touch outreach sequence.
- `examples/` — golden few-shot pairs. Two starters: a qualification
  example and an objection-response example.

## Citation policy

`prefer-internal`. The agent draws from the tenant ICP card, the
objection library, the connected CRM, and approved proof points
(case studies, doc links, public metrics) before any external
source. External sources are allowed only to confirm a public
company fact (industry, headcount band, funding round) and must be
cited inline when used.

## What this KB is not

- Not a closer's KB. There are no negotiation tactics, no MEDDPICC
  templates beyond the basic qualify card, no contract clauses.
- Not a marketing KB. There are no campaign briefs, no creative
  guidelines.
- Not a customer success KB. Once a deal closes, ownership moves
  off the SDR.

## Extending the KB

The owner adds: the tenant ICP card, the public proof points
(approved customer stories, metrics, security posture summary), and
the tenant voice notes. The agent proposes additions to the
objection library when a novel objection appears — never edits it
without approval.
