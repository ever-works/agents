# Example: Objection response — good response

## User input (prospect reply to touch 1)

```
From: Marcus Pham (VP Engineering, Northbridge Health)
Channel: email, reply to touch 1
ICP score: 4 / 5
Touch 1 trigger: their public hiring page added 6 platform-eng roles
Touch 1 ask: 20-minute call to walk through how Ever Works handles
on-call rotation handoffs.

Reply body:
"Appreciate the note. Honestly the price page on your site already
puts you outside what we'd consider for a side-channel tool. We're
going to stick with what we have for now."
```

## Good agent response

```
Objection response set
----------------------

Class: price (with a hint of fit — "side-channel tool")
Reframe: The price-page tier you're seeing is the seat-based SaaS
  shape — for a platform-eng team your size, the relevant package
  is the platform plan, which is priced per workload not per seat
  and lands meaningfully lower.
Proof: Northbridge-shaped customers (mid-size platform-eng teams
  consolidating on-call tooling) typically come in at the platform
  tier — see the published case study on Lumeo's rollout for the
  pricing shape (link in KB: proof/cases/lumeo-platform-plan).
Ask: 15-minute call this week — I can walk through the platform-
  tier shape and you can decide if it stays out of scope.

Full reply text:
  Marcus, fair callout — the seat-based tier on the page is not
  the relevant one for a platform-eng team at Northbridge's size.
  The platform plan is priced per workload and is the shape that
  Lumeo's team chose for the same on-call consolidation problem
  (case study linked in the signature). If the platform-tier
  numbers still look out of scope, we close this thread cleanly.
  Either way, 15 minutes this week?

  Case study: <KB link to Lumeo platform-plan case>

  --
  Sales SDR, Ever Works agent for Ever Works
```

## Why this is good

The agent classified the objection correctly (price, with a fit
hint), did not invent a discount, cited a real KB proof point
(Lumeo case study) rather than a fabricated customer, made exactly
one ask, and offered a clean exit ("we close this thread cleanly")
without guilt-trip language. The signature identifies the sender as
an Ever Works agent.
