# Example: Qualify a lead — good response

## User input (lead capture)

```
Source: demo request form
Captured: 2025-11-12 09:42 UTC
Name: Anna Briggs
Role: Head of Operations
Company: Loomspark Logistics
Email: anna.briggs@loomspark.example
Stated reason: "We're hiring three more dispatchers next quarter
and our scheduling spreadsheet is on its last legs. Want to see how
your scheduling automation handles 24/7 shifts."
Company web: 80-person logistics firm, UK + NL, Series B announced
last month.
```

## Good agent response

```
Lead card
---------

Prospect: Anna Briggs (Head of Operations at Loomspark Logistics)
Source: demo request form        Captured: 2025-11-12 09:42 UTC
CRM record: <to be created>

ICP score: 4 / 5

Matches:
- Segment: mid-market logistics (ICP target).
- Headcount: 80, inside the 50-250 band.
- Geography: UK + NL, both in ICP.
- Budget signal: Series B announced last month — funded.

Gaps:
- Decision-maker: Head of Operations is the buyer in our ICP for
  ops-led purchases, but for scheduling automation we usually also
  loop in Head of Workforce — not present here.

Trigger: Public Series B announcement two weeks ago plus stated
plan to hire three dispatchers next quarter — capacity expansion
under a funded company.

Recommendation: qualify

Rationale: Strong ICP fit on segment, size, geography, and budget
signal. The stated reason names a concrete pain (spreadsheet at end
of life) and a concrete timeline (next quarter). The only gap is
co-buyer presence — surface this in the first call rather than the
first email.

Suggested next step: send touch 1 referencing the Series B + 24/7
shift scheduling pain, ask for a 20-minute call.
Due: 2025-11-13 (within 24 hours of capture).
Channel: email
Owner approval required before send: yes
```

## Why this is good

It scores explicitly against ICP dimensions, names the trigger from
public data, identifies one real gap (missing co-buyer) without
inventing a problem, and proposes one concrete next step with a due
date. The agent did not draft the outreach in this task — that is a
separate task.
