# Checklist: Pre-send outreach

Run this checklist before any outreach message leaves the agent.
Every item is pass/fail. A single fail blocks the send.

## Identity and honesty

- [ ] The signature line identifies the sender as an Ever Works
  agent for {{tenant}}.
- [ ] No claim of being a human anywhere in the message.
- [ ] No fabricated mutual connection, prior conversation, or
  shared customer named in the body.
- [ ] No invented metric, customer logo, or quote.

## Fit and relevance

- [ ] The prospect has an ICP score recorded in the CRM.
- [ ] The message references the specific trigger or signal — not a
  generic "I saw your company".
- [ ] The role on the envelope matches the actual decision-maker
  pattern for this segment, or there is a recorded reason for the
  override.

## Form

- [ ] Touch 1 is under 90 words. Touch 2 is shorter than touch 1.
- [ ] Exactly one ask in the body. Not two, not "and also".
- [ ] Subject line is under 60 characters and not all-caps.
- [ ] No false-urgency language ("today only", "last chance",
  "limited spots").
- [ ] No guilt-trip language ("if you'd prefer never to hear from
  us again").

## Channel and consent

- [ ] The prospect has not previously opted out on any channel.
- [ ] The channel matches the agent's per-prospect channel setting
  — no multi-channel without owner toggle.
- [ ] If LinkedIn, the source is the connected approved feed — not
  scraped or logged-in scraping.

## CRM hygiene (post-send)

- [ ] CRM next-step is set with a due date 3-5 business days out.
- [ ] Stage is correct ("contacted" after touch 1).
- [ ] The send is logged on the deal record.

## Fail handling

If any item fails, do not send. Surface the failing item to the
owner with a one-line reason. Do not "fix" by softening the rule —
fix by rewriting the message or by escalating.
