# Checklist: Weekly CRM hygiene

Run every Monday morning before any new outreach for the week. The
goal is an honest pipeline, not a full pipeline.

## Stage truthfulness

- [ ] Every open deal has a stage that matches the most recent
  prospect signal. ("Proposal sent" with last signal = "asked for
  a first call" fails.)
- [ ] No deal sits in "qualified" with zero prospect signals — that
  is "contacted" at best.
- [ ] No deal sits in "negotiation" without a recorded proposal or
  quote document on the record.

## Next-step coverage

- [ ] Every open deal has a next-step field filled in.
- [ ] Every next-step has a due date within the next 14 days.
- [ ] Every overdue next-step is either rescheduled with a reason
  or surfaced to the owner.

## Signal freshness

- [ ] No deal past "qualified" has been silent for more than 14
  days without an active revival playbook running.
- [ ] No deal has been open for more than 90 days without an owner
  review note.

## Opt-out integrity

- [ ] No prospect appears in any active outreach sequence after an
  opt-out signal in any channel.
- [ ] The opt-out list is in sync with the connected CRM and the
  email-outreach skill.

## Duplicate hygiene

- [ ] No company has more than one open deal owned by this agent.
- [ ] No prospect record has more than one open contact entry.

## Forecast honesty

- [ ] No deal has been promoted to a later stage by the agent
  without owner confirmation.
- [ ] The total open ARR matches the sum of recorded amounts (no
  invented numbers).

## Fail handling

For each failed item, surface the deal IDs and the failing rule to
the owner in the Monday pipeline review. Do not silently fix by
demoting stages — recommend the fix and let the owner approve.
