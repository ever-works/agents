# Checklist: Brand voice self-edit

Run this pass on every draft before delivery. Each item is pass or
fail. If any item fails, fix the draft or note why the rule was
bent.

## Sentence shape

- Sentences are short. Median sentence under 18 words.
- Active voice dominates. Passive voice only when the actor genuinely
  does not matter.
- Second person addresses the reader ("you"). Third person describes
  the product ("the product", "the agent", not "we" unless the
  brand-voice doc explicitly allows "we").
- No exclamation marks unless the brand-voice doc permits them.
- No emojis.

## Word choice

- No empty intensifiers: powerful, seamless, robust, world-class,
  cutting-edge, next-gen, revolutionary, game-changing.
- No filler verbs: leverage, utilize, empower, unlock. Replace with
  the actual action verb.
- No vague nouns where a concrete one fits: "solution" → the product
  name; "team" → the role; "process" → the named workflow.
- Industry jargon only when the audience already uses it. If you had
  to look it up, the reader will too.

## Claims

- Every benefit traces to a feature in the product KB.
- Every number, customer name, or testimonial traces to a source in
  the KB.
- Unverifiable claims are marked `[UNVERIFIED: ...]` inline, not
  silently dropped or quietly shipped.
- No aspirational claims framed as fact ("soon", "imagine",
  "coming") unless the KB confirms a roadmap commitment.

## Structure

- One job per asset. The asset asks for one action.
- Headlines do not repeat in the body. The body extends the
  headline; it does not restate it.
- CTA verb matches the action ("Start free trial", not "Learn
  more" when the action is signup).
- Length matches the constraint in the brief.

## Forbidden

- No scare tactics or false urgency.
- No competitor names outside `/compare/*` assets.
- No lifted wording from competitor sites or ads.

If three or more items fail, the draft is not ready. Rewrite before
delivery.
