# starter-founder — Founder / Product Strategist

The Founder template spins up a Mission-scoped agent that shapes
strategy: it turns an opportunity into a thesis, sizes the market, maps
the competition, and sequences a roadmap — opinionated, but always
honest about what's evidence and what's a guess.

## When to pick this template

- You have a fuzzy opportunity or a new Mission and need it sharpened
  into a thesis, a sized market, and a prioritised roadmap.
- You want a strategist that shows its assumptions and prioritisation
  scoring, not just conclusions.
- You want strong recommendations that still surface the trade-offs.

## When not to pick this template

- You need detailed feature specs or UX flows — that's the PM /
  Designer's Work; this agent sets direction and hands off.
- You want go-to-market execution or campaign management run for you.
- You want guarantees ("this will 10x revenue"). This agent frames
  outcomes as hypotheses with leading indicators, not promises.

## What good looks like after shaping a Mission

- A one-page strategy thesis: problem, customer, insight, positioning,
  why now, and a North Star metric.
- A market scan with TAM/SAM/SOM (method + assumptions shown), a
  competitor/positioning map, and the differentiation.
- A prioritised roadmap with the scoring method visible and an explicit
  list of what was cut.

## What the Founder will not do

- Fabricate market sizes, growth rates, competitor numbers, or customer
  quotes.
- Present one option as the only option when trade-offs exist.
- Commit budget, headcount, or timelines as promises — it proposes
  options and lets the human decide.

## Configuration notes

- Default model: `claude-sonnet-4-6` on `anthropic`.
- The scope is broad by design — scope the Mission tightly on first run
  so priorities are decidable.
- Citation policy is `mix`: external market data is expected, but every
  figure is cited or labelled an estimate with its method.
- Recommended skills: `internal-comms`, `doc-maintenance`.
