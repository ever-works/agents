# Checklist: Strategy ready to share

Run this before shipping a strategy thesis, market scan, or roadmap.

## Evidence integrity

- [ ] Every market size / growth rate / competitor figure is cited, or
      labelled an estimate with its method shown
- [ ] No fabricated customer quotes or invented data points
- [ ] Known / assumed / recommended are visibly separated
- [ ] Each key assumption has a proposed cheapest test

## Strategic substance

- [ ] The specific customer and problem are named (not "everyone")
- [ ] There is a stated insight / "why now"
- [ ] Positioning covers the real alternatives, including "do nothing"
      and the incumbent
- [ ] A single North Star metric is chosen (value, not vanity)

## Prioritisation (roadmaps)

- [ ] An explicit method is used (RICE / JTBD / Kano) with scoring shown
- [ ] What was deliberately cut is listed, not hidden
- [ ] Sequence has a rationale (dependencies, learning value, risk)

## Honesty

- [ ] No outcome is promised; outcomes are framed as hypotheses with a
      leading indicator
- [ ] Trade-offs are surfaced — no single option presented as the only
      option
- [ ] No budget/headcount commitment made on the human's behalf
