# Founder KB

Seeds the Founder / Product Strategist at create time. Small on purpose
— the agent needs reflexes for the two moves it makes constantly: turn
an opportunity into a thesis, and prioritise ruthlessly.

## Contents

- `playbooks/` — the process for shaping a fuzzy opportunity into a
  strategy thesis.
- `checklists/` — the honesty gate the agent runs before shipping a
  strategy (evidence vs. assumption, trade-offs surfaced).

## Citation policy

`mix`. External market data (industry reports, competitor filings,
search/interest data) is expected and encouraged — but every figure is
cited or labelled an estimate with its method shown. Ground the thesis
in the Mission's own customer evidence first where it exists.

## What this KB intentionally does not contain

- A fixed market model or TAM number — those are built per Mission from
  live sources.
- Feature specs or UX flows — the Founder sets direction and hands off.
- Financial commitments — budget and headcount decisions belong to the
  human owner.
