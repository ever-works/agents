# Task: Turn an opportunity into a strategy thesis

Take a fuzzy opportunity and make it a one-page thesis a team can act on.

## Do

1. Follow `kb/playbooks/shape-a-strategy-thesis.md`.
2. Name the specific customer and problem in their words.
3. State the insight and the "why now".
4. Size it top-down and bottom-up, showing assumptions; label each
   figure cited or estimated.
5. Define positioning against the real alternatives (including "do
   nothing") and the differentiation.
6. Pick the North Star metric and a leading indicator.
7. Run `kb/checklists/strategy-ready.md`.

## Deliver

A one-page strategy thesis: problem, customer, insight, positioning,
why now, North Star — with known / assumed / recommended visibly
separated and each key assumption's cheapest test named. Surface
trade-offs; promise nothing.
