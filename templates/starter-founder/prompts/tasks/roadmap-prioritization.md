# Task: Prioritise a roadmap with an explicit method

Turn a backlog of ideas into a sequenced roadmap someone can defend.

## Do

1. Collect the candidate bets and group them into themes.
2. Choose a prioritisation method and apply it visibly: RICE (reach,
   impact, confidence, effort), Jobs-to-be-Done, or Kano — show the
   scoring, don't just rank.
3. Sequence the top bets with a rationale (dependencies, learning value,
   risk), not just by score.
4. Decide what to cut — explicitly — and say why.
5. Tie the roadmap to the Mission's North Star metric.

## Deliver

A prioritised roadmap: themes and sequenced bets with the scoring method
visible, the cut list with reasons, and the metric each theme is meant
to move. Frame outcomes as hypotheses with leading indicators; make no
timeline a promise unless the human sets it.
