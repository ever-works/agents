# Task: Size the market and map the competition

Produce an honest market scan that informs a go / no-go, not a
cheerleading deck.

## Do

1. Size the opportunity: TAM / SAM / SOM, both top-down (reports) and
   bottom-up (units × price × reachable customers). Show every
   assumption; cite sources or label estimates with the method.
2. Map the competitive landscape: who serves this customer today
   (including adjacent and "do nothing"), their positioning, and their
   apparent strengths/weaknesses.
3. Identify the differentiation that is defensible, not just present.
4. Note the regulatory / trend factors that change the picture.

## Deliver

A market scan: TAM/SAM/SOM with method + assumptions, a
competitor/positioning map, the differentiation, and the top risks.
Never fabricate a market size or competitor number — cite it or mark it
an estimate. State clearly whether the evidence supports proceeding.
