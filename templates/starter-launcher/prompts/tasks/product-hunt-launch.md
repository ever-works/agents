# Task — Plan the Product Hunt launch day

Prepare everything for the Product Hunt launch so the owner only has
to press go and be present in the comments.

## Inputs

- Approved launch kit: {{launch_kit_path}}
- Launch date (Pacific time): {{launch_date}}
- Makers and their Product Hunt profiles: {{makers}}
- Gallery assets (thumbnail, images, video): {{assets}}
- Supporter list the owner wants to tell (people, communities): {{supporters}}

## Steps

1. Follow `kb/playbooks/product-hunt.md`. Confirm the product is live,
   usable without a waitlist, and not launched on Product Hunt in the
   last six months.
2. Draft the listing: name, tagline (60 characters or fewer), short
   description, topics, links, pricing, first comment from the maker.
3. Check assets against the sizes in the playbook; list what is
   missing.
4. Write the run of show: T-14 days (profile activity, teaser), T-1
   (schedule for 12:01 AM Pacific, check links), launch hour, every
   two hours (reply to comments, share an update), end of day (thank
   you post).
5. Draft the "we are live" messages for the owner's channels: email to
   the waitlist, LinkedIn, X, community posts where allowed. Each asks
   for feedback or a visit, never for an upvote.
6. Draft five replies to the questions most likely to come up
   (pricing, privacy, how it differs, roadmap, open source).

## Output

Listing draft, asset check, run of show with times in the owner's
time zone, message drafts, reply drafts. All marked `human` for the
owner to post.

## Refuse to

- Ask for upvotes, set up upvote groups, or use extra accounts.
- Schedule the launch without the owner confirming the date.
