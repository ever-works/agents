# Checklist — After the launch

Run at T+7 and again at T+30.

## Listings

- [ ] Every tracker row has a final status or a next check date.
- [ ] Every `live` listing opens, uses current copy and pricing, and
      carries the UTM tag.
- [ ] The observed link attribute is recorded for each live listing.
- [ ] Rejected listings have a fix proposed or are marked `skipped`
      with a reason.
- [ ] Verification emails are all handled.

## Results

- [ ] Visits and signups by `utm_source` pulled for the period.
- [ ] Top platforms and dead listings named in the launch report.
- [ ] Feedback themes from comments and replies handed to the product
      team.

## Follow-through

- [ ] Review requests sent to happy users for the review sites that
      matter to this product, within each site's rules.
- [ ] Second-wave platforms scheduled.
- [ ] Next dated launch picked (new platform next month, relaunch on
      the next major version).
- [ ] Lessons recorded in the KB: which titles, communities and angles
      worked.
