# Checklist — Launch readiness

Run before planning any submission. Mark each item pass, fail or not
applicable, with the evidence. A failing item in "Product" blocks the
dated launches; failing items elsewhere block the platforms that need
them.

## Product

- [ ] A new user can reach the core value in one session, starting
      from the public URL, without talking to anyone.
- [ ] Signup asks for no more than an email or a single sign-in
      button (or there is no signup at all).
- [ ] There is a free tier, a trial, a demo or a download people can
      try now. Required for Show HN and Product Hunt.
- [ ] The product works on the platforms the kit claims.
- [ ] At least a handful of real users are happy with it (retention
      before reach).

## Website

- [ ] Landing page states what it does, for whom, and the price in the
      first screen.
- [ ] Pricing page or pricing line, matching the kit.
- [ ] Privacy policy and terms pages (directories and stores ask).
- [ ] Contact email that someone reads.
- [ ] Social preview image (1200x630) and page titles set.
- [ ] Sitemap submitted to search consoles; robots rules do not block
      search or assistant crawlers by accident.

## Tracking

- [ ] Analytics on the landing page and the signup flow.
- [ ] A signup or activation event the report can count.
- [ ] UTM parameters survive the signup flow (stored on the account or
      the first event).

## Assets

- [ ] Square logo 512x512 PNG and a 240x240 export.
- [ ] Three to five screenshots at 1270x760 or larger.
- [ ] Demo video under two minutes, hosted on a video site (optional
      but strongly recommended).
- [ ] Maker photo and short bio for launch platforms.

## Open source (if applicable)

- [ ] Public repository with README (one-line pitch, screenshot or GIF,
      quick start), license file, topics, website link.
- [ ] Container image or release binaries published.
- [ ] Self-hosting docs: one command or one compose file to start.

## Launch logistics

- [ ] Launch kit approved by the owner.
- [ ] Launch inbox (alias) receiving verification emails.
- [ ] List of accounts needed and who creates each.
- [ ] Owner available on launch day and the Show HN day.
