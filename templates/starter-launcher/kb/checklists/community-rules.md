# Checklist — Community post rules

Run for every community post draft before it goes to the owner.

## Rules

- [ ] The community's current rules page was opened today; the draft
      follows its self-promotion rules (designated thread, day, flair,
      ratio of promotion to participation).
- [ ] The owner has the history the community expects, or the post is
      marked `wait` with a participation plan.
- [ ] The post is written for this community. It is not the same text
      as another community's draft.
- [ ] Affiliation is clear: the owner says they built it.

## Content

- [ ] The title is a hook (problem, lesson or result), not an ad.
- [ ] The first lines give value without clicking: what was learned,
      what problem it solves, real numbers the owner agreed to share.
- [ ] A demo (video, GIF or screenshots) is included where the
      community allows media.
- [ ] Limitations and pricing are stated plainly.
- [ ] No marketing words (revolutionary, game-changing, best ever).

## Behaviour

- [ ] Nothing asks for upvotes, likes or reposts.
- [ ] No plan to use other accounts, groups or paid engagement.
- [ ] The reply plan covers the first hour and the three most likely
      questions, including a hostile one.
- [ ] The post is spaced from the owner's other community posts (one
      per community per day at most).
