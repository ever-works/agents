# Golden example — Launch kit for a fictional product

The product, people and numbers below are invented for the example.

## User input

> "We're launching Pacer next month: open-source time tracking for
> small agencies, self-host it with Docker or use our cloud at $6 per
> user. It replaces the paid trackers agencies hate. Can you write the
> launch kit?"

## Good response (excerpt)

**Launch kit — Pacer** (status: draft)

**Audience**

> "Helps agency owners at 5-50 person studios who lose billable hours
> to forgotten timers get accurate invoices every week, without paying
> per-seat fees for a tool they could host themselves."

- Trigger event: a client disputes an invoice, or the tracker bill
  rises after a price change.
- Where they hang out: r/agency, r/selfhosted, agency-owner Slack
  groups, LinkedIn.

**Taglines**

1. Open-source time tracking your agency can host itself (53)
2. Track billable hours without per-seat tracker fees (50)
3. Time tracking and invoices for small agencies, open source (58)

**One-liner (ten words)**

Open-source time tracking and invoicing for small agencies, fully self-hostable.

**50 words**

Pacer is open-source time tracking for agencies of 5 to 50 people.
Timers, timesheets, client projects and invoices in one place. Run it
on your own server with one Docker command, or use Pacer Cloud at $6
per user per month. No per-seat lock-in: the code is MIT licensed.

**Features**

- Timers in the browser, desktop app and CLI
- Weekly timesheets with manager approval
- Client and project budgets with alerts at 80 percent
- Invoices generated from approved time
- One-command self-hosting with Docker Compose

**Alternatives**: the two paid trackers the owner named.

**Open questions for the owner**

- The draft says "MIT licensed". Confirm the license file matches.
- No customer numbers are in the copy. Share any you want public.

## Why it is good

- Every claim is checkable in the product or the repository.
- Taglines name the outcome and stay under 60 characters (counts shown).
- The kit asks instead of inventing numbers.
