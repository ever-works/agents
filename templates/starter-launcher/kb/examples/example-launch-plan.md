# Golden example — Launch plan excerpt for the fictional product

Product: Pacer (open-source, self-hostable time tracking for small
agencies; web app, desktop app, CLI, Docker image). Invented example.

## Good response (excerpt)

**Platforms**: 41 (9 auto, 21 assisted, 11 human). Owner time: about
6 hours over 5 weeks.

### Long-lead (start T-30)

| Platform id     | Why it fits                                                                         | Automation | Who   | Cost      |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- | ----- | --------- |
| docker-hub      | Self-hosters start from the image page                                              | auto       | agent | free      |
| capterra        | Agencies compare time trackers here; one vendor profile also feeds two sister sites | assisted   | Dana  | free      |
| g2              | Buyers in the time-tracking category read reviews here                              | assisted   | Dana  | free      |
| microsoft-store | Desktop timer for Windows users                                                     | assisted   | Dana  | check fee |
| flathub         | Desktop timer for Linux users, open source fits                                     | auto       | agent | free      |

### Launch day (T0, a Tuesday)

## Why it is good

- Every row says why it fits; off-topic categories are excluded with a
  reason.
- Show HN is on a different day from Product Hunt.
- Paid items are listed, not bought.
