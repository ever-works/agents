# Playbook — Show HN

A Hacker News front-page day can bring hundreds of technical users.
The comment section is the main event.

## Eligibility (Show HN rules in short)

- It is something you made and people can try now.
- No signup wall, email gate or waitlist in front of the core use; a
  demo, a free tier, a download or a repository is fine.
- Not a landing page, a fundraising announcement, a list or a blog
  post about a product (those are normal submissions, not Show HN).
- Read the current Show HN guidelines on the site before posting.

## The post

- Title: `Show HN: <Name> - <what it does in plain words>`. No
  superlatives, no exclamation marks.
- Link to the thing itself: the product, the repository or a plain
  walkthrough video.
- First comment from the maker: why it exists, how it works, the hard
  parts, what is not done, what feedback is wanted. Technical depth is
  welcome here; long form is fine.
- Drop all marketing language. Readers notice it at once.

## Timing and presence

- A weekday morning, US Eastern time.
- The maker stays in the thread for at least three hours, then checks
  back through the day. Reply to every substantive comment; ask
  follow-up questions; keep the discussion going.
- Expect criticism, and expect the top comment to be negative. Answer
  calmly and specifically, concede what is true, hold the line on what
  is not. The silent majority reads the replies.

## Never

- Ask anyone to upvote or comment, or share the link in groups to get
  votes. The site detects vote rings and kills the post.
- Post from more than one account, or repost the same thing within days.

## Timing within the launch

Do it early. The product does not need to be mature, only real. Put
it on a different day from the main launch event so the maker can give
each thread full attention.
