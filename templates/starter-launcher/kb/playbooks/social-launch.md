# Playbook — Social launch (LinkedIn, X, video)

The owner posts; the agent drafts, times and prepares the replies.

## LinkedIn

Fits products for founders, startups, tech companies and developers.
For pure consumer products, expect less.

- There is no title. The first sentence has one job: earn the
  "see more" click. An outcome or a surprising number works.
- Personal and career stories outperform announcements. Two formats
  work: short punchy lines that read as a scroll, or a strong two or
  three paragraph story (harder, bigger payoff).
- Add a video or image; it takes more space in the feed.
- Tag people you can justify tagging: early users, collaborators,
  people who inspired the build. Tag the company page in the post.
- End with a question to invite comments.
- Reply to every comment as soon as the post is live. Early engagement
  decides reach.

## X

- Reposts decide reach. Optimise for them above likes.
- A post can go wide with a small following if it gets around a
  hundred quality reposts. Build relationships with people who
  genuinely reshare launches in the category, long before launch day.
- A well-made launch video beats text. Budget the time or money for it,
  or cut it yourself. Keep raw, low-budget founder videos for Reddit
  and Hacker News, where authenticity wins.
- Build-in-public threads: show a before and after, tag the few
  relevant accounts, post in the US morning.

## Short video (TikTok, Reels, Shorts)

"Day X of building" works: 15-30 seconds showing the product doing
the job. No heavy editing needed. Consistency beats production value.

## Quora and Pinterest

Slow but compounding. On Quora, answer the questions buyers search for
and link a genuinely helpful page (a guide or comparison), disclosing
affiliation. On Pinterest, visual products pin templates, screenshots
and infographics that link to useful pages.

## Never

- Buy followers, reposts or engagement, or use engagement pods.
- Post from the owner's account without the owner.
