# Playbook — Product Hunt launch

Product Hunt visitors are actively looking for new products, so intent
and conversion are high. A product gets about one launch every six
months, so it is worth preparing properly.

## Eligibility

- The product is live and usable now, without a waitlist in the way.
- It has not launched on Product Hunt in roughly the last six months,
  or it has a substantial new version.
- The maker has a real profile with some activity.

## Two to four weeks before

- Maker profile complete; the maker comments on and follows other
  launches for about 30 days. Followers are notified at launch.
- Build the supporter list: users, beta testers, communities the owner
  belongs to, people who said they want to hear about it.
- Optional: ask an active, trusted member to hunt it. Self-hunting is
  fine and common.
- Prepare assets: thumbnail 240x240 (an animated GIF works), gallery
  images 1270x760, a first gallery slide that explains the product in
  seconds (usually a short video), a launch video if available. Both
  polished and low-budget videos have worked; the idea matters more.

## Listing

- Name, tagline (60 characters or fewer, outcome-first), short
  description, topics (pick the most specific ones), links, pricing.
- The maker's first comment: who the makers are, why they built it,
  what is in it, an honest note on what is next, and the feedback
  wanted. Posted at go-live.
- A launch offer for the community, if the owner approves one.

## Launch day

- Launches go live at 12:01 AM Pacific. Tuesday to Thursday have the
  most traffic and the most competition; weekends are quieter and can
  be easier to place on.
- Product Hunt curates what is featured on its homepage; a strong
  listing and real engagement improve the odds, nothing guarantees it.
- Get the core team on a call in the first hours and reply to every
  comment, all day. Engagement and replies matter.
- Tell the supporter list, Slack and Discord groups the owner belongs
  to, LinkedIn connections and the waitlist that the launch is live.
  Ask for feedback or a visit. Never ask for upvotes, never use upvote
  groups or extra accounts: the platform detects and penalises them.
- Post short updates during the day on the owner's channels.

## After

- Thank-you post with what was learned and what ships next.
- Add the launch badge to the website only if the owner wants it.
- Move feedback into the product backlog.
- Record rank, comments and signups by `utm_source=product-hunt` in
  the tracker and launch report.
