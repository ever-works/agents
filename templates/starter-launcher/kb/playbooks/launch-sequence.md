# Playbook — Launch sequence (T-30 to T+30)

Use this to place every platform in the plan on a date. It is the
default; the owner can move things.

## Principles

- **Retention before reach.** A launch pours people into the product.
  If fewer than about ten users are genuinely happy with it, fix that
  first; launching a leaky product burns the one-time launch slots.
- **Run both kinds of channel.** Unscalable channels (launch events,
  communities, direct outreach) bring the first users in weeks.
  Scalable channels (search, content, integrations, paid when approved)
  take three to six months to work. Start the scalable ones on day one
  so they are ready when the launch spike fades.
- **Launches are research.** Each launch tests a message on a new
  audience. Record which title, community and angle worked, and reuse
  it in the next one.
- **A cadence beats one big day.** A focused launch on a new platform
  every month (three to four days of work each) compounds: momentum,
  social proof, and a sharper picture of the buyer.
- **Do not spread thin.** For communities and social, pick the two or
  three places the buyer actually reads and be consistent there.

## Foundations (before T-30)

Write the audience sentence before anything else:

> "Helps [job title] at [type of company] who struggle with [pain] get
> [outcome] in [time frame], without [main objection]."

Then answer: where these people spend time online, the words they use
for the problem, and the trigger event that makes them look for a
tool now (just raised, just hired, just launched, audit coming). The
answers drive platform choice and copy.

## Waves

| Wave          | When              | What goes here                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ------------- | ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Long-lead     | T-30, in parallel | App store and marketplace reviews, browser extension review, vendor profiles on review sites, integrations (automation marketplaces), developer program sign-ups, catalog pull requests that need maintainer review. These take days to weeks.                 |
| Pre-launch    | T-30 to T-7       | Readiness check, launch kit, accounts the owner must create, the Product Hunt maker profile and activity, waitlist and beta-tester communities, a supporter list (users, friends, communities the owner belongs to), package registries and repository polish. |
| Final prep    | T-7 to T-1        | Assets final, dated launch scheduled, community drafts reviewed, tracker created with every planned row, UTM links generated, "we are live" messages drafted.                                                                                                  |
| Launch day    | T0                | One dated launch event (usually Product Hunt). The owner's social posts and an email to existing users or the waitlist. Nothing else dated on the same day.                                                                                                    |
| Launch week   | T+1 to T+5        | Show HN on a different weekday than the main launch. Community posts spread one per day. First directory batch: easy startup directories, software directories, AI or agent directories if they fit (10-20 per day).                                           |
| Weeks 2-4     | T+6 to T+30       | Second launch boards with longer visibility windows, review-site profiles and the first review requests, newsletter pitches, awesome-list and catalog pull requests, technical articles on developer blogs.                                                    |
| Ongoing       | after T+30        | Review collection, milestone posts, content that answers buyer questions, one new launch platform a month, relaunch on major updates.                                                                                                                          |
| When eligible | later             | Platforms whose requirements the product does not meet yet (enterprise review sites, catalogs that need months of release history). Keep them in the plan with the condition.                                                                                  |

## Order of the dated community launches

When the product is new and the message is untested, this order works
because each step informs the next:

1. **Reddit** in a few fitting subreddits. Cheap message testing; the
   subreddit that responds tells you who the buyer is. Expect the
   first 20-30 users, not hundreds.
2. **Hacker News (Show HN)** early, not last. The product does not need
   to be polished, only real and tryable. A front-page day can bring
   hundreds of users for technical products.
3. **LinkedIn** if the buyer is a founder, a developer, a startup or a
   tech company. One strong personal post can drive real trials.
4. **X** for products with a visual demo. Reposts decide reach.
5. **Product Hunt** once the message is proven and assets are polished;
   it is limited to about one launch per product every six months.
6. **Press** last, as an amplifier: a headline to quote in posts, not a
   signup source.

Rough targets used for planning, not promises: about 50 upvotes in the
right subreddit, front page and about 200 points on Hacker News, top
five of the day on Product Hunt, 200K impressions for a strong LinkedIn
post, 500K impressions on X with around 100 quality reposts.

## Seven-day sprint (small launches)

| Day | Work                                                                                                              |
| --- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1-2 | Audience sentence, readiness check, launch kit, accounts list.                                                    |
| 3   | Product page polished, sitemap submitted to search consoles, analytics and UTM in place, first content page live. |
| 4-5 | Two or three community posts, the owner's social posts, outreach to the warm list.                                |
| 6-7 | Review replies and feedback, adjust the message, submit to 10+ fitting directories, count signups by source.      |
