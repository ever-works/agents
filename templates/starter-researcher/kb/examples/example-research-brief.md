# Example: Research brief — golden output

A worked example of the Research brief format. Use as a few-shot
anchor for structure, citation density, and tone. The content is
illustrative; do not copy the citations as if they were verified.

## User input

> What is the current evidence on whether four-day work weeks
> increase or decrease productivity? I need to brief leadership next
> week. Focus on the last three years and on knowledge-work settings,
> not factory floors.

## Good response

```
Question: Does a four-day work week increase or decrease productivity
in knowledge-work settings, based on evidence from the last three
years?

Answer: Recent trials report neutral-to-positive productivity outcomes
in knowledge-work settings, but the strongest evidence comes from
self-selected employers, which limits how far the result generalises.
No trial in the window reports a clear productivity decline.

Evidence:
  1. The 4 Day Week Global UK trial (2022) reported revenue stable
     or up across 61 participating organisations after six months.
     [1][2]
  2. Iceland's earlier large-scale trial (2015-2019) found similar
     output with shorter hours, but the sample was public-sector and
     pre-dates the three-year window. [3]
  3. Self-selection is a recurring critique: employers who opt into
     trials are already culturally primed for the change. [4]
  4. No randomised trial in knowledge work has been published in the
     window. [5]

Confidence: medium — the headline finding is supported by multiple
sources, but the strongest data is from self-selected employers and no
randomised trial exists.

What I couldn't verify:
  - Long-term (5+ year) productivity effects. No source in the window
    reports past the 12-month mark.
  - Sector-specific differences inside "knowledge work" (e.g.
    consulting vs software). Sources aggregate.

Related but out of scope:
  - Effects on employee retention and wellbeing — separate question,
    different evidence base.

Sources:
  [1] 4 Day Week Global UK Pilot Results · 4 Day Week Global ·
      2023-02-21 · https://example.org/4dwg-uk-results ·
      61-org trial; revenue stable or up.
  [2] UK four-day-week pilot final report · Autonomy Institute ·
      2023-02-21 · https://example.org/autonomy-report · Independent
      write-up of the same trial.
  [3] Going Public: Iceland's journey to a shorter working week ·
      Alda / Autonomy · 2021-06-04 · https://example.org/iceland ·
      Public-sector trial, output unchanged.
  [4] The four-day week is being oversold · The Economist ·
      2023-03-10 · https://example.org/economist-critique ·
      Self-selection critique.
  [5] Search of registered trials returned no randomised study in
      knowledge-work settings in the 2023-2026 window. (Negative
      result.)
```

## Why this is good

- Answer is two lines, not a paragraph.
- Every load-bearing claim has a citation.
- Confidence is `medium` with a one-line reason.
- `What I couldn't verify` is present and concrete.
- A negative search result is cited as such — not glossed over.
