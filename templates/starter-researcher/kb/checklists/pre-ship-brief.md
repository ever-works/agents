# Checklist: Pre-ship research brief

Run this checklist on every brief before returning it. Each item is
pass or fail. Fix the failures; do not paper over them.

## Structure

- [ ] The brief starts with a one-line `Question`.
- [ ] The `Answer` is one to three lines, not a paragraph.
- [ ] `Evidence` is a numbered list, each item with at least one
      inline citation marker.
- [ ] `Confidence` is `high`, `medium`, or `low`, followed by a
      one-line reason.
- [ ] `What I couldn't verify` is present, even if the value is
      "nothing — everything was verified".
- [ ] `Sources` is the final block, one source per line, in the
      format `title · publisher · date · URL · takeaway`.

## Citations

- [ ] Every load-bearing sentence in the body has at least one
      citation.
- [ ] Every inline `[n]` marker has a matching entry in the Sources
      block.
- [ ] No source in the block is unused in the body. If unused, cut.
- [ ] At least two independent sources back every claim labelled as
      `Fact`. Claims with only one source are labelled `Opinion` or
      `Single-source`.

## Wording

- [ ] No quote marks around paraphrased text.
- [ ] No undefined jargon. Technical terms are translated to plain
      English the first time they appear.
- [ ] No marketing language ("revolutionary", "game-changing",
      "best-in-class").
- [ ] No hedging filler ("it could be argued", "some say"). If
      uncertain, say "I am uncertain" with a reason.

## Scope

- [ ] The brief answers the question that was asked.
- [ ] Broader questions implied by the asker but not answered are
      listed in a `Related but out of scope` line, not silently
      addressed or silently dropped.

## Honesty

- [ ] If the agent could not answer, the brief says so. It does not
      pad with related-but-not-asked content to avoid an empty
      answer.
- [ ] If sources disagree, the brief reports the disagreement. It
      does not pick a winner without explaining why.

If three or more items fail, the brief is not ready. Fix and re-run.
