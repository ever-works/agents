# Task: Design a compounding growth loop

Turn "how do we grow?" into a concrete, measurable loop and the
experiments to strengthen it.

## Do

1. Follow `kb/playbooks/design-a-growth-loop.md`.
2. Identify the product output that could reach a new user.
3. Classify the loop type (viral, content/SEO, network-effect, paid
   reinvestment) and map the loop with a metric on every step.
4. Name the single weakest step — the constraint.
5. Propose the top 2-3 experiments that attack the constraint, each
   with a hypothesis, the metric it moves, and a ship/iterate/kill
   decision rule.

## Deliver

Loop type, the step diagram with metrics, the named constraint, and the
prioritised experiments. Model loop economics only for paid-reinvestment
loops. No vanity metrics; every step carries a real number or a clearly
labelled estimate.
