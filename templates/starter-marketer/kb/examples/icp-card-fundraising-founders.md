# Golden example — ICP card for "Founders prepping for fundraise"

## User input

> "We keep getting traction with early-stage SaaS founders but I
> can't tell if that's a real segment or just noise. Can you turn
> this into an ICP? Here are six interviews and our last 90 days
> of intercom tickets in the KB at `kb/research/2026-q1/`."

## Good response

**ICP Card — Founders prepping for fundraise**

Status: draft
Owner: Ruslan
Last updated: 2026-05-22

**Who they are**

Solo or two-cofounder SaaS teams, $20k-$200k ARR, six-to-eighteen
months out from raising a priced seed round. They are not yet
"venture-backed" but they intend to be.

> "I have eight weeks before I want to start partner meetings and
> my deck still has a placeholder for 'why now'."
> — Interview I-04, kb/research/2026-q1/i-04.md

**Job to be done**

Walk into a partner meeting with a story that does not collapse
under five minutes of due-diligence questions about the market.

**Trigger event**

A scheduled or planned intro to a VC partner in the next 60 days.
Five of six interviewees named a specific calendar event.

**Where they hang out**

- Online: r/SaaS, r/startups, Lenny's Newsletter, Indie Hackers,
  the South Park Commons Slack.
- Offline: SaaStr Annual, On Deck weekend events, local YC alumni
  dinners (for the YC subset).
- Trusted sources: Lenny Rachitsky, Jason Lemkin, the Acquired
  podcast.

**What they read before buying**

Pitch-deck breakdowns, "what investors actually ask" posts,
specific firm partner blogs (a16z, Bessemer SaaS index).

**Disqualifiers**

- Bootstrapped founders who explicitly do not want VC money.
  Same demographic, opposite JTBD.
- Series A-and-later founders. They have a fundraising team and
  do not buy positioning help from a tool.
