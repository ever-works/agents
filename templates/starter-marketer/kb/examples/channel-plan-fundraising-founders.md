# Golden example — Channel plan for "Founders prepping for fundraise"

## User input

> "Now that we have the ICP card and a draft message ladder for
> the fundraising-founders segment, propose a channel plan. We
> have ~$3k/month of marketing spend and four hours/week of
> founder time. Test window: eight weeks."

## Good response

**Channel Plan — Founders prepping for fundraise**

Linked ICP card: kb/segments/fundraising-founders.md
Linked message ladder: kb/segments/fundraising-founders-ladder.md
Test window: 2026-06-01 to 2026-07-27 (8 weeks)
Next review: 2026-06-29 (mid-point)

Three channels. Ranked by compounding potential.

---

**1. Long-form "why now" teardowns (asset-building, owned)**

- Why this segment: Five of six interviewees read pitch-deck
  breakdowns and "what investors ask" posts. The watering hole
  is search and newsletter syndication, not social.
- Leading metric: Email signups from the gated companion
  checklist on each teardown.
- Target: 40 signups by week 4; 100 by week 8. Set against
  Lenny's archive benchmark (a single mid-traffic post on a
  comparable topic averages 60 signups in two weeks).
- Smallest test: Publish two teardowns. One in week 1, one in
  week 4.
- Kill criterion: If fewer than 20 signups by 2026-06-29, drop
  this channel and reallocate the founder hours.

**2. Targeted reply guy on r/SaaS and r/startups**

- Why this segment: Same five-of-six interviewees named these
  subreddits as where they lurk during fundraise prep.
- Leading metric: Profile clicks to the teardown post + replies
  that ask to see the checklist.
- Target: 30 profile clicks/week sustained from week 2 onward.
- Smallest test: 30 minutes/day, weekdays only, week 1 through
  week 3.
- Kill criterion: If profile clicks fall below 10/week by
  2026-06-29, drop.

**3. Paid LinkedIn boost on the first teardown (paid, ranked
last)**

- Why this segment: LinkedIn is NOT the watering hole, but it
  is where the segment is most identifiable. Used here only to
  amplify the asset built in channel 1.
- Leading metric: CPM and click-through to the teardown.
- Target: $25 CPM ceiling, 1.5% CTR floor.
- Smallest test: $500 of the $3k budget, one week, one
  teardown.
- Kill criterion: If CTR is below 0.8% after $200 spend, kill
  and refund the rest of the budget to channel 1.
- Owner approval required before spend.

---

**Rethink trigger**

> "I would change this plan if interviews next month show that
> founders are buying via warm intro from their existing
> investors — that makes content irrelevant and shifts the plan
> toward a referral program."
